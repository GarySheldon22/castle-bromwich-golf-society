Castle Bromwich Golf Society

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Castle Bromwich Golf Society respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable UK data protection law.

This privacy policy explains what personal information we may collect, why we use it, how we protect it, and the rights individuals have in relation to their personal information.

1. Who we are

Castle Bromwich Golf Society is responsible for deciding how and why personal information relating to the society's members and activities is used.

Privacy contact:
Gary Sheldon
Castle Bromwich Golf Society
Email: GaryJSheldon@gmail.com

2. What personal information we may collect

Depending on your relationship with the society, we may hold some or all of the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Postal address where required
  • Golf handicap information
  • Competition and match information
  • Competition results and scores
  • Society honours and achievements
  • Event attendance information
  • Payment information where required for society activities
  • Other information voluntarily provided to the society

3. How we use personal information

We may use personal information for legitimate society activities, including:

  • Managing society membership
  • Organising golf fixtures and competitions
  • Maintaining playing handicaps
  • Communicating with members about society activities
  • Organising society events
  • Recording competition results
  • Recording society honours and championship results
  • Managing payments and contributions where necessary
  • Responding to enquiries
  • Managing the general administration of the society
  • Complying with legal or regulatory obligations where applicable

4. Our lawful basis for using information

Data protection law requires organisations to have a lawful basis for using personal information.

Depending on the circumstances, Castle Bromwich Golf Society may rely on:

  • Legitimate interests – where using information is necessary for the proper administration and running of the golf society and does not unfairly override an individual's rights.
  • Consent – where we ask for consent for a specific use of information.
  • Legal obligation – where the society is required to use or retain information to comply with a legal requirement.

Where we rely on consent, individuals may withdraw their consent at any time.

5. Golf competition results and honours

The society may publish information about golf competitions and society achievements. This may include a member's name, competition result, score, placing, handicap or society honour.

Competition results and historical honours may be retained as part of the society's sporting records and history.

Where there is a specific concern about the publication or continued display of personal information, please contact Gary Sheldon using the details provided above.

6. Who we may share information with

Personal information will only be shared where there is a legitimate reason to do so and where appropriate safeguards are in place.

Depending on the circumstances, information may be shared with:

  • Society committee members who need the information to carry out their duties
  • Golf clubs and venues when required to organise competitions or events
  • Service providers who assist with society administration
  • Relevant authorities where we are legally required to provide information

We do not sell members' personal information.

7. Website

The Castle Bromwich Golf Society website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The main society website contains a basic password gate intended to restrict casual public access to members' information.

This password gate should not be considered a high-security system. Information that is particularly sensitive should not be placed on the website unless appropriate protection is in place.

This privacy policy is intentionally available publicly and does not require the Members Area password.

8. Cookies

The society website is intended to operate without the use of non-essential cookies for advertising or tracking purposes.

The underlying hosting service may process technical information as part of providing and securing the website. Such processing may be governed by the relevant service provider's own privacy information.

9. How long we keep personal information

We will keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or where a longer period is required by law.

Some information relating to historical competitions, results and society honours may be retained for legitimate historical and sporting record purposes.

10. How we protect personal information

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

Access to personal information should be limited to people who need it for legitimate society purposes.

11. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, individuals have rights under UK data protection law, including rights to:

  • Be informed about how personal information is used
  • Request access to personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request erasure of personal information in certain circumstances
  • Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Object to certain types of processing
  • Request data portability where the right applies
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

These rights are subject to certain conditions and exemptions under data protection law.

12. How to exercise your rights

If you would like to ask a question about your personal information, exercise a data protection right, or raise a concern about how your information is being handled, please contact:

Gary Sheldon
Castle Bromwich Golf Society
GaryJSheldon@gmail.com

Please provide enough information for us to identify you and understand your request. We may need to verify your identity before providing personal information.

13. Complaints

We hope that any questions or concerns can be resolved by contacting us first.

Individuals also have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), if they believe their personal information has been handled unlawfully.

Information about making a complaint can be found on the Information Commissioner's Office website .

14. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in the society's activities, website or applicable data protection requirements.

The date at the top of this page will be updated when significant changes are made.
